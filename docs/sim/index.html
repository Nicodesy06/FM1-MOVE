START

Press PLAY after loading the demo, or play the keys. SELECT picks the track, the top row of buttons opens the sound and mix screens, the bottom row the time and arrangement ones.

KEYBOARD

Z…M, Q…Uthe keys (two octaves, chromatic)
F1…F6FX · SEL · ENV · LFO · EDIT · GLO (with HOLD on: a press latches the button, a second press lets go)
F7…F10HOME · SAVE · ARP · SEQ (F11 / F12 are the browser's: use Space / Enter for PLAY / REC)
Space EnterPLAY · REC
← → / ↑ ↓SELECT (track) / ALGORITHM (on PLAY, TRACKS, MIXER, PERFORM: the tempo)
[ ]OCT− · OCT+ (both together, tapped in time: tap tempo)
Knobs: drag up / down or use the wheel. Hold a button and press keys for its layer (hold F10 / SEQ: the steps page; F2 / SEL: the chords on the keys).

WHAT THIS IS

The FM1 Move firmware (eight-track engine, Groove Engine, clips and scenes, the STEP editor, controls, screens, projects) running unchanged on a simulated panel. Saved projects stay in this browser. It is not the hardware: timing, audio load and the screen's speed differ, and nothing here has been tested on an FM-1 yet.
