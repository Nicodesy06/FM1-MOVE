FM1 Move

An eight-track groovebox for the M-VAVE FM-1, with SLOOP 2.6 inside. Any track can be a synth or a drum machine, every pattern can breathe with the Groove Engine, and every function is one press away.

Quick startTry it in the browserInstall & manage
Beta, experimental. The maintainer tests FM1 Move in simulation (host tests and the browser simulator, which runs the firmware's own code). Community testers have installed earlier 0.3 builds on their FM-1s and reported bugs; this build's fixes are verified in simulation only and wait for their retest. There has been no structured hardware test pass yet (0.3.1: what was fixed, what to retest). Read Install and Recovery before you put it on your device.

Quick start 5 MINUTES

From a fresh install to your first eight-track loop. Everything here also works in the browser simulator (keyboard: Z…M and Q…U are the keys, F1…F10 the buttons, Space PLAY, Enter REC).

  1. Switch on and press PLAYA new project already has eight tracks: drums on 1 and 7, bass, chords, lead, pad, pluck and keys on the others. The PLAY light flashes on every beat. Press PLAY again to stop.
  2. Pick a track, play itTurn SELECT — one detent, one track (or hold SEL and press white key 1–8). The keys now play that track. Turn PRESETS for another sound (on a drum track: another kit).
  3. Write a beat without recordingSelect track 1 (drums). Play the kick once on the keys, then hold SEQ and tap white keys 1, 5, 9, 13. Play the snare, hold SEQ, tap 5 and 13. Tap the same key again to remove a step.
  4. Record a basslineSelect track 2, press PLAY, then REC: what you play is recorded at once and every pass adds on top. Press REC again to stop recording. Made a mistake? EDIT + OCT− undoes it.
  5. Give it a groovePress ARP (the GROOVE screen). Turn ALGORITHM for a template (MPC 16, HOUSE, UK GARAGE…), KNOB 1 for swing, KNOB 4 for a human feel.
  6. Mix and performPress GLO (MIXER): KNOB 1–4 are volume, pan, reverb, delay of the selected track. Hold GLO and press keys 1–8 to mute, 9–16 to solo. Hold FX and press a key for a punch-in effect. On PLAY, ALGORITHM turns the tempo.
  7. Keep itStop, leave the FM-1 alone 2.5 s: the autosave keeps your work, even through a power-off. To keep a version: hold HOME (PROJECT), pick a slot with ALGORITHM, press SAVE.

Tempo NEW

The tempo is always one hand away, with no menu: turn it, tap it, or set it exactly.

  • Turn it: on PLAY, TRACKS, MIXER and PERFORM — the screens with nothing to navigate — turn ALGORITHM, no button held. One detent is one BPM; fast turns go further. The BPM shows in large numerals for about 1.5 s.
  • Tap it: press OCT− and OCT+ together, in time, a few times. Pressing both still puts the octave back to 0, as before. GLO held + black key A# taps too.
  • Exactly, from any screen: hold any function button and turn SELECT, one BPM a detent (as on SLOOP).
  • From the computer: the device page, Global › BPM.

When SETTINGS › CLOCK IN is USB or TRS, the tempo follows the incoming MIDI clock: the readout says FOLLOWS CLOCK IN and its numerals are dimmed.

Every track is independent and has no fixed role. Each one is a synth or a drum track (any number of drum kits at once), with its own pattern, length, division, level, pan, sends, filter, groove and mute / solo. Each track has its colour everywhere.

Change a track's kind

On TRACKS (SEL), turn KNOB 1: the screen asks "TRACK 2 TO DRUMS? — its pattern is cleared". Press SEL to confirm, anything else to cancel. All six of the track's clips are emptied too (synth steps and drum lanes do not convert).

Heavy engines

The GRAIN, FM6 and PHYS engines each need a big memory area; three are available. A fourth heavy engine is refused with a message ("HEAVY ENGINES ALL IN USE") until one of them changes engine; a project that asks for more loads them as ANALOG and says "HEAVY ENGINES: 3 MAX". Choosing a GRAIN, FM6 or PHYS sound then leaves the track's sound as it was (before this update it loaded a different sound of the old engine).

Voices and heavy load

The synth tracks share 8 voices. Three heavy engines can be loaded, but that does not mean any mix of them plays without glitches at any voice count: PHYS (MODAL, bowed and SYMP models especially) costs about twice an ANALOG voice, FM6 and GRAIN about 1.3×, and inserts, DIST and track filters add to it. When the FM-1 measures that its audio has run late (two audio buffers in a row over 85 % of their time), it fades out one voice (the quietest releasing, else the oldest held, never a track's lowest note or lead) and holds the voice limit one lower, so new notes do not refill it at once; the limit comes back one voice a second while nothing runs late (never under 4). This only happens under real overload, and is a deliberate trade: a voice less instead of a click. HOME held twice › SETTINGS › AUDIO LOAD shows the highest load of the audio since power-on (% of its time) and how many audio buffers came late: "LATE 0" means the audio never ran out of time.

Mute, solo, mix

Hold GLO: keys 1–8 mute tracks 1–8, keys 9–16 solo them. The MIXER screen (tap GLO) sets the selected track's volume, pan, reverb and delay sends; choose the track with SELECT.

The sequencer

Up to 64 steps per track, each track with its own length and division — polymeters stay in phase on one sample-accurate clock, with no drift.

Write steps (no recording needed)

  1. Select the track, play the note or chord you want (on a drum track: the sound) — or hold SEQ and turn KNOB 1 to pick it.
  2. Hold SEQ and tap white keys 1–16: each writes that step of the page. Tap a lit step again to clear it.
  3. Longer patterns: with SEQ held, OCT− / OCT+ or the first four black keys change the page; on the SEQUENCER screen, ALGORITHM.

While SEQ is held the keys' lights show the steps of the page and the playhead.

Edit a step

Hold SEQ, then hold a step's key (or several), and turn:

The STEP page NEW

SLOOP's step editor, back: on the SEQUENCER screen of a synth track, press SEQ again (the page dots show page 2; SEQ once more goes back). The drum track keeps its grid only.

The top shows the cursor's 16 steps as a piano roll over the pattern's note range (black-key rows, a line on every C), the cursor as a raised column with a white ▲, the playhead tinted. Under it, one cell per step: a note is filled, a tie is a half bar, a rest is empty, an accent is a cap, a slide is a ramp to the next note. The title reads "STEP n / length".

Pattern tools

  • Length and division: SEQUENCER KNOB 3 (1–64 steps) and KNOB 1 (1/32 to 2 bars, triplets included).
  • Swing: SEQUENCER KNOB 2, MPC style, 0 (straight) to 100.
  • Shift: SEQUENCER KNOB 4, or EDIT held + KNOB 1.
  • Length ×2 / ½ and transpose: EDIT held + KNOB 2 / KNOB 3.
  • Erase: EDIT held + the key of a note or drum sound removes it as the loop plays it; stopped, from the whole pattern.
  • Fills: steps marked "fill only" play only during a fill; GLO held + F# plays a fill while held, + G# fills the next bar.
  • Mute drum sounds live: on a drum track, hold EDIT and press a black key: the white keys now mute and unmute their sounds in the sequencer (nothing erased). A black key again, or letting go of EDIT, goes back to erasing. The mutes apply to every drum track.
  • Undo / redo: EDIT + OCT− / OCT+.

Chords and the key

Steps hold chords of up to four notes. Hold SEL: white keys 9–16 and the black keys set the key of the song; the knobs set chord mode (one key plays a triad, 7th, 9th, sus4 or power chord of the scale), the scale (16, from major and minor to blues and whole tone), key snapping (off, snap, scale on the white keys) and transpose. In chord mode the black keys change the chord while it is held. While SEL is held the KEY page shows the chord every white key plays.

SCALE LOCK NEW

As Ableton’s scale mode: pick a key and a scale, and the whole composition stays in it — every synth track, not the drums.

  1. Hold SEL: white keys 9–16 or the black keys set the root; KNOB 2 the scale (16: major, minor, dorian, mixolydian, pentatonics, harmonic and melodic minor, phrygian, lydian, locrian, blues, whole tone, diminished…).
  2. Still holding SEL: KNOB 3 SCALE LOCK = LOCK (every key into the scale, a key out of it plays the scale’s note below) or WHITE (the white keys walk the scale from C = the root). It applies to every synth track at once, like the scale.
  • The keys light the scale: its notes glow, the roots are lit, the keys you hold light up, the notes out of the scale stay dark.
  • Editing stays in the scale: NOTE on the STEP page, SEQ + KNOB 1 and a held step's NOTE move from scale note to scale note; a MIDI keyboard's notes on the STEP page are put into it. The piano roll shades the rows out of the scale and its title reads the key (C MIN 1 / 16).
  • FIT TO SCALE: SEL + OCT− moves the notes already in the selected track's pattern into the scale (rounded down, as the keys); EDIT + OCT− undoes it.
  • With chords: chord mode builds its chords from the same scale, so every chord is in the key; the white keys play them, the black keys still change them (CHORD+).

Recording

  • Playing: REC starts the take on the next bar's first beat (its light blinks while it waits), and REC during a take lets it finish the bar before it stops — never in the middle, as Ableton's quantisation. SETTINGS › REC QUANT sets the cycle: 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars (counted from PLAY), or OFF to record at once. REC again while it waits cancels. Every pass is added on top (overdub). Notes land where you heard them — the keys' latency is taken back.
  • Stopped: REC arms (its light flashes); your first note — or PLAY — starts the loop and the recording.
  • Stop recording: REC again ("REC OFF").
  • Metronome and count-in: HOME held twice › SETTINGS › METRONOME (OFF · REC · ON) and COUNT-IN (OFF · 1 BAR).
  • Undo a take: EDIT + OCT− (one step; EDIT + OCT+ redo).
  • Note repeat: hold ARP and hold keys: rolls at the rate of KNOB 1, recorded as ratchets.
  • Drums with dynamics: hold OCT− / OCT+ while you play for ghost / hard hits; every hit keeps its level.
  • Timing is kept: the recorded deviation of every note stays with it, so the Groove Engine's quantize can be changed at any time.
The REC screen options of SLOOP (free tempo, length) keep their stored values but have no FM1 Move page yet (the count-in is in SETTINGS), and holding REC to clear a track is gone — use EDIT held + keys while stopped, or undo.

Clips and scenes NEW

Each track keeps six patterns, its clips; the one it plays is the pattern you see and edit. Change clips — or all eight tracks at once with a scene — on the next bar, without stopping.

Launch

  • A clip: hold SAVE (CLIPS) and press white key 1–6: clip 1–6 of the selected track.
  • A scene: hold SAVE and press white key 9–14: scene 1–6 puts every track on its clip 1–6. A track whose clip is empty goes quiet.
  • When: while playing, the new clip starts at the start of the next bar on its first step, whatever its length and division; stopped, at once. Press PLAY to stop before the bar and the queued clip is there at once.

While SAVE is held the key lights show the clips: keys 1–6 lit = playing, blinking = queued for the next bar, dim = holds a pattern, off = empty; keys 9–14 lit = that scene plays (every track on that clip), dim = some track holds that clip.

How clips keep your work

  • Everything that writes a pattern — SEQ + keys, recording, EDIT, the STEP page, the groove, the device page — writes the clip playing. Switching clips first keeps what the outgoing clip got, so nothing is lost.
  • Clips are saved with the project and the autosave.
  • The clips share 8 KB of memory; a 16-step pattern takes about 100–300 bytes. When it is full the panel says CLIP MEMORY FULL: the clip keeps its last stored version, and the pattern playing is still saved with the project. Clear clips you no longer need to make room.
  • Changing a track between synth and drums empties its clips.

The Groove Engine NEW

Quantize without losing your timing, templates of famous feels, swing, micro timing and a human touch — all non-destructive, per track or for the whole song.

ControlGROOVE screen (ARP)
ALGORITHMTemplate: STRAIGHT, MPC 16, HOUSE, UK GARAGE, BROKEN, ROLLING, LATIN, USER. Choosing one also sets its swing.
KNOB 1Swing, 0–100
KNOB 2Quantize strength: 100 % on the grid, 0 % exactly as played, anything between (how much, which grid)
KNOB 3Groove amount: how much of the template's timing and accents
KNOB 4Humanize: small timing and velocity variations
  • Non-destructive: what you played is kept; quantize, template and humanize act as it plays and can change any time, even while playing.
  • Per track or song: tracks follow the song's groove until you edit a track's groove — then it has its own.
  • Humanize repeats: the variations are the same every loop and after saving, so a take you like stays as it is.
  • Swing once: swing shapes the grid; templates add only what swing does not, so nothing is moved twice.

How much quantize, on which grid

  • Strength, per track: ARP (GROOVE) › KNOB 2 QUANT. 100 % puts every recorded note on the grid; 75 % / 50 % / 25 % move it that share of the way from where you played it; 0 % plays it exactly as played. A new track starts at 100 % (as SLOOP). Each track keeps its own value, and the project saves it.
  • Your timing is never thrown away: each recorded note keeps how early or late you played it (up to half a step), so you can lower or raise the strength at any time, even after saving and loading.
  • The grid is the track's step: TRACKS (SEL) › KNOB 4 DIV, 1/32 to 2 bars, triplets included. To record on an 1/8 grid, set DIV 1/8 before recording (DIV also sets how fast the pattern plays, so set it first).
  • Not the same as REC QUANT: SETTINGS › REC QUANT only decides when a take starts and stops (on 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars). It never moves notes.
  • What stays on the grid: note lengths (a note lasts whole steps) and ratchets. Only the start of a note keeps your timing.
Fixed in this update (host simulation): the free first take of an empty project now keeps each note's timing, like any other take (it used to land fully on the grid whatever the strength); an overdub on a step no longer replaces the timing of the notes already on it; a step written by hand no longer inherits an old take's timing.
Commit (baking the groove into the steps, with one undo) is implemented and tested in the engine but has no panel gesture yet.

Sounds and engines

Twelve synth engines and 153 factory sounds, all level-matched, plus 37 drum kits — SLOOP's sound, all of it.

Engines

ANALOG · DIGITAL (4-op FM) · PHASE · LOFI · SAMPLE · VOICE · TRIO · WHEEL (organ) · GRAIN · FM6 (6-op, DX7 patches) · PHYS (physical models) · NOISE

Per sound

Envelope, LFO, arpeggiator, glide and voice modes (poly, mono, legato, unison), drive, slicer, chorus / delay / reverb sends, a filter per track

Drums

37 kits (a sampled acoustic kit in 5 treatments, 32 synthesised from 808 to techno), SYN1–4 synthesised user kits, USR1–3 sample kits; 16 sounds on the white keys

  • ENV and LFO jump straight to those pages. Pages without a picture show their four values large.
  • PLAY shows the macros too, so the sound is always under your hands.

Saved with the project.

Some parameters have no panel page yet. The device page reaches every parameter of every track: the eight engine parameters, envelope destinations, the rest of the LFO, the slicer, the arpeggiator, voice mode and glide, strum and voice leading, the global delay, reverb and chorus, and the tempo.

Master

PERFORM (FX): DJ filter (low-pass ← off → high-pass), DUST (an old sampler and a record: bits, rate, crackle), DUCK (the kick pumps the synths), and the selected track's filter. An output limiter that looks 0.7 ms ahead keeps the mix safe without crackle. FX held + OCT− / OCT+: reverb and echo throws (see Layers).

Projects and saving

  • Autosave: stop and leave the FM-1 alone for 2.5 s: the working project is kept in flash (at most every 20 s). At power-on FM1 Move comes back exactly as you left it.
  • Two project slots: hold HOME for PROJECT. ALGORITHM picks the slot; SAVE saves (asks before overwriting), EDIT loads (asks, the working project is replaced), REC deletes (asks). Stop the transport first.
  • Format: FM1M — eight tracks and their six clips each, every parameter, the steps that hold anything, locks, nudges, fills and the groove. A save cut off by power loss leaves the previous version intact.
  • From an earlier FM1 Move build with four slots (before clips): project 1 and the autosave still load (each track's pattern becomes its clip 1); projects 2–4 do not carry over (slot 2 shows empty). Before updating, export the projects you want to keep from the device page.
  • From FM1 Move 0.2: projects, settings and backups load in 0.3 as they are; the new SLOOP 2.6 parameters (INSERT, TEXTURE, sync…) start off.
  • From SLOOP: at the first start, SLOOP's working project is imported onto tracks 1–4. SLOOP's four saved slots are not imported yet.
  • On the computer: the device page exports and imports single projects (.fm1m.json) and makes a full backup.

The battery NEW

The status bar shows the battery as a cell with three bars, left of the Q and G badges: 3 bars FULL, 2 GOOD, 1 LOW, none EMPTY (the empty cell turns orange). When it gets empty a BATTERY LOW toast says so once; it says it again only after the battery has been charged. SETTINGS › BATTERY gives the same reading in words.

With the FM-1 on a computer's USB the bars fill one after another and SETTINGS says ON USB: the USB powers it, and the FM-1 tells us no charge state we can read, so FM1 Move does not claim one. The levels are SLOOP's (the same battery reading and thresholds SLOOP shows); there is no percentage because the reading is not that precise. Not yet checked on a real FM-1 running FM1 Move. The browser simulator has no battery: it always shows a full one.

MIDI and USB

  • USB MIDI in and out, class compliant (no driver), and the 3.5 mm TRS MIDI IN jack (with a TRS-to-DIN adapter).
  • Channels: channel n plays synth track n (1–8), and track n sends on it; drum tracks use the drum channel (10, changeable on the device page), which plays track 1; any other channel plays the selected track. (SLOOP 2.6's CH1–CH3 settings do not apply in FM1 Move.)
  • MIDI CCs: 7 level, 10 pan, 74 filter, 71 resonance, 73 / 75 / 72 attack / decay / release, 5 glide, 91 / 93 / 94 the reverb, chorus and delay sends.
  • Sustain pedal (CC 64) NEW: holds the notes you let go while it is down, recorded as you hear them.
  • Pitch bend NEW: bends the track the channel plays, by up to BEND semitones (1–12, device page › MIDI 2).
  • MIDI IN on one channel NEW: device page › MIDI 2 › IN = CH 1 … 16 answers that channel only, on the selected track. The panel's SETTINGS › MIDI IN shows NOTES / CLOCK only.
  • The MIDI settings (drum channel, IN, OTHER, BEND) belong to the FM-1, not to the project: loading a project leaves them as they are.
  • Clock in: USB or TRS (SETTINGS › CLOCK IN), pulse by pulse with no drift.
  • USB audio: the FM-1 is also a USB audio input (44.1 or 48 kHz stereo) — record it in a DAW or a phone over the same cable.

MIDI LEARN NEW

  1. Hold GLO and press black key C# (C#4, the fourth black key): MIDI LEARN is on.
  2. Turn a knob of the FM-1 (a sound or mix parameter of the track), then move a knob of your controller: that CC is learned. Turn the next knob, and so on.
  3. GLO + C# again turns it off.

Up to 16 CCs, kept through power-off with the settings. In FM1 Move a learned CC moves its parameter on the selected track, like the panel's knobs: select another track and the same controller knob plays that track's parameter. The mod wheel, the sustain pedal and the like cannot be learned (CC NOT LEARNABLE).

FM1 Move has no panel item to forget the learned CCs yet (SLOOP has HOME › SYSTEM › MIDI LEARN CLEAR).

Program Change (SLOOP 2.6) loads a sound on the track its channel plays: the n-th sound of the PRESETS list, kit n on a drum track. On the STEP page (SEQ again), a MIDI keyboard on the selected track writes the cursor's step as the FM-1's keys do: notes held together make a chord, letting go moves on.

The device page

nicodesy06.github.io/FM1-MOVE/device — Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 on a USB data cable. Every tab also works on the virtual FM1 (the firmware running in the browser), without a device.

Install

FM1 Move 0.3.1 (beta, behind a warning you must tick), SLOOP 2.6 (stable), or back to the official V15 with M-VAVE's own file.

Tracks & sounds

The eight tracks; engine, sound or kit; every parameter of the track and the song (those the panel does not reach are marked "web only").

User presets

Store a track's sound in one of 32 slots, load, rename, erase, export and import them.

Samples & kits

Three sample slots (USR1–3, about 7.4 s each: mono, 22.05 kHz, 4-bit IMA ADPCM). Build a drum kit by dropping sounds on 16 pads, or a multi-sampled instrument — WAV, MP3, AIFF…

FM6 patches

Load DX7 cartridges or single voices (.syx) into the bank B1–B27; export the bank.

Projects & backup

Load, save, export, import and clear projects; one file backs up everything and puts it back.

Install

  1. Open the device page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 directly with a USB data cable; close other apps that use MIDI.
  2. If your FM-1 runs SLOOP and you have work on it, save a backup with SLOOP's editor first (FM1 Move reads SLOOP's working project, not its backup files).
  3. Go straight to Install — you don't need Connect to install, whatever firmware the FM-1 runs now (Connect is for managing an FM-1 that already runs FM1 Move). Read the warning, tick it, press Install FM1 Move, allow MIDI when the browser asks. Do not unplug while it writes.
  4. The FM-1 restarts into FM1 Move. Connect it on the page to see its version.
FM1 Move is a beta: community testers run it on their FM-1s, but no structured hardware test pass has been made, and each update's fixes are verified in simulation first. Install it only on a device you are ready to recover. Installing custom firmware is at your own risk.

Coming from SLOOP

  • FM1 Move 0.3 is built on SLOOP 2.6: every engine, sound and sequencer feature is still there, including 2.6's 27 punch-ins, throws, INSERT, LFO sync, ENV SYNC, note LEN and velocity, TEXTURE, live drum mutes, MIDI LEARN, sustain and pitch bend. The panel is new and has eight tracks of any kind.
  • SEL held + keys 1–8 now selects tracks (the song key moved to keys 9–16 and the black keys); GLO held: keys 1–8 mute, 9–16 solo; black keys F# fill, G# fill next bar, A# tap tempo, C# MIDI LEARN (SLOOP: GLO + F#3).
  • Tempo: ALGORITHM on PLAY, TRACKS, MIXER and PERFORM, or OCT− + OCT+ tapped together; button held + SELECT as on SLOOP.
  • MIDI: track n on channel n (SLOOP 2.6's GLO › MIDI page is not on the panel); a learned CC acts on the selected track; Program Change and MIDI-keyboard step entry work as in SLOOP 2.6.
  • INSERT on four tracks at once at most (memory).
  • SLOOP's song mode (sections A–D, chains, SONG REC) is gone; clips and scenes replace it (six clips per track, six scenes, launched on the bar).
  • SLOOP's layer pages for SEQ (STEPS, 4 × 4) and SEL (the chords on the keys) are back: hold the button.
  • SLOOP's STEP page is back as the SEQUENCER's STEP page (SEQ again), with accent and slide.
  • Sample slots: three (USR1–3). SLOOP's USR4 flash now holds FM1 Move's projects.
  • The visualiser, colour palettes, zoom and the calibration page are gone from the panel; SLOOP's offline editor and song-section erase are SLOOP-only.

Questions

Can I write a sequence without recording?

Yes: hold SEQ and tap the white keys — see The sequencer.

How do I change the tempo?

On PLAY, TRACKS, MIXER or PERFORM, turn ALGORITHM — no button needed; the BPM shows large. Or tap OCT− + OCT+ together in time. See Tempo.

How do I set an exact tempo?

Hold any function button and turn SELECT (one BPM a detent) from any screen, or set BPM on the device page. GLO held + A# taps it too.

Why does a track's INSERT do nothing?

Four tracks can have an insert effect at once; the panel says "INSERT: 4 TRACKS MAX". Turn another track's INSERT off.

Why does a fourth GRAIN / FM6 / PHYS track not get its engine?

Three heavy engines can run at once; change one of them to free its memory.

I hear a glitch with PHYS, FM6 and several other tracks

Please tell us, with the project. Right after a glitch, look at SETTINGS › AUDIO LOAD: if LATE is above 0 the audio ran out of time (see Voices and heavy load); if it is 0 the cause is elsewhere. Fewer inserts, track filters or PHYS voices lighten the load.

How do I clear a track?

SAVE, REC, REC on the selected track — see Clear a track.

Can quantize be gentler?

Yes, per track: ARP › KNOB 2, from 100 % (on the grid) to 0 % (as played) — see How much quantize.

Can one step have its own filter?

Yes: hold SEQ and the step's key, turn ALGORITHM to the filter (the track FILTER, or the sound's filter) and PRESETS to its value for that step. Up to 24 locks per track.

How do I switch between patterns while playing?

Hold SAVE and press white key 1–6 (a clip of the selected track) or 9–14 (a scene, every track): it changes on the next bar — see Clips and scenes.

Where are my SLOOP projects?

The working one is imported at the first start. The four saved slots stay in flash, untouched; importing them is planned.

Does the device page send my files anywhere?

No. Everything happens between the browser and the FM-1 over USB; the virtual FM1 keeps its data in your browser only.
